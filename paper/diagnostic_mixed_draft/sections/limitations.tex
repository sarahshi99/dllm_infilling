\section{Limitations}
We do not claim SOTA, frozen-test improvement, model-agnostic causal mechanisms, or a completed standalone method. The 1033-span SingleLine results are development evidence after repeated inspection. The 6707-span audit derives from 148 HumanEval base tasks and lacks its final cluster-aware statistical package. MultiLine and RandomSpan remain HumanEval variants rather than real repository-level software engineering. Official CAL, rho-EOS, and DreamOn are not yet reproduced under one protocol, while LR-DLLM lacks an executable local adapter. Non-recovery under the tested oracle action does not uniquely identify the backbone as the cause.
