\section{Discussion}
The results make the research question sharper but do not yet yield a solid CCF-A method paper. There is substantial candidate and oracle headroom, yet simple deployable selection is harm-prone. Structure features appear informative, while a hand-weighted proxy is not reliably better than all simpler signals. This motivates genuine program-state obligations rather than another threshold or weighted-score variant.

The empirical track and method track must proceed together. Official CAL and at least one of rho-EOS or DreamOn need protocol-level comparison; the 6707 spans need base-task clustering; and a non-HumanEval, repository-oriented benchmark is required for FSE realism. None of these gaps is replaced by implementing another method.
