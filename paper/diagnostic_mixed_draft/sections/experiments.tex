\section{Experiments}
The evidence base includes H200-replayed LLaDA SingleLine development results, action-ceiling and controller validation, Dream-Coder full allowed SingleLine diagnostics, official HumanEval MultiLine/RandomSpan first-pass and full-allowed stress runs, and a 148-task Phase 5 shared candidate bank. The early 1033-span population was repeatedly inspected during V1--V8 and Route2 development and is not a held-out result. The 6707-span full-allowed population contains only 148 independent base-task clusters.

Official CAL, rho-EOS, and DreamOn are not yet locally reproduced under a common protocol; local CAL-lite is not an official CAL result. LR-DLLM remains code/adapter-blocked. The frozen controller test is sealed and has evaluation count zero. A non-HumanEval repository-context benchmark has not yet been run.
