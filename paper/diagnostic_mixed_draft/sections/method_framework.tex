\section{Evidence Framework}
We separate diagnostic oracles, deployable controls, and method hypotheses. Fixed64 and CAL-lite are internal controls; oracle-sufficient canvas estimates action-specific headroom and is never deployable. Controller V1--V3 are validation-only route-closure evidence. Phase 5 evaluates inference-visible structure on a shared candidate bank: its fixed AST/def-use proxy is a premise diagnostic (M1-D0), not the full Abductive Program-State Bridge.

Four future methods remain independent: Abductive Program-State Bridge, Constraint-Homotopy Infilling, Birth--Death Canvas Diffusion, and Semantic Particle Assembly. Metamorphic equivariance is an auxiliary diagnostic rather than a correctness oracle. Exploratory implementations require only technical integrity gates; paper promotion additionally requires fresh grouped validation, compute-matched baselines, cost and harm accounting, and external transfer.
