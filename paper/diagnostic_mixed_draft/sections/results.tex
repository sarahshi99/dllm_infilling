\section{Results}
On the full allowed second-regime population, fixed64, CAL-lite, and oracle-sufficient canvas pass 2019, 1464, and 3180 of 6707 spans, respectively. CAL-lite uniquely helps 620 spans but harms 1175 control passes; oracle canvas gains 1633 and harms 472. These are descriptive span totals pending cluster-aware analysis over 148 base tasks.

Dream-Coder full allowed SingleLine produces 735/927 primary, 744/927 simple-policy, and 858/927 oracle-canvas passes, showing transferable recoverability but a model-dependent boundary. In Phase 5, the fixed structural proxy reaches 0.6273 cross-canvas pairwise accuracy and selects 15 and 14 net additional passes over fixed64 and confidence selection, but fails the preregistered requirement for positive grouped-bootstrap lower bounds against every deterministic baseline. The current proxy is therefore not promoted.
